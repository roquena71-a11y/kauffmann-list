Kauffmann List · working draft, 7 November 2026

Every plaque, every name, every story.

In preparation. Not published. No charitable status is claimed. No partnership has been agreed.

What this is

A free, non-commercial map of the Stolpersteine in Germany, founded by Carlos Kauffmann. A family anywhere should be able to search a name or an address and see the plaque, with what can be sourced. A longer biography of the founder is not published here: there is no source for one.

What exists today

64,576 stones. OpenStreetMap, ODbL, 4 November 2026; Wikidata, CC0, 7 November 2026. Incomplete: Stiftung Spuren counts over 116,000 stones in 31 countries; open data for Germany are short by an estimated 40,000.

50,636 photos (Commons, 78.4%). 60,943 inscriptions, unchanged. 4,038 biography texts on the map, Wikipedia CC BY-SA only, and only on a certain match of name, place and address or photo. About 9,900 longer texts sit in the lists; uncertain matches are not shown.

Link only, no text: 24,451. Official pages, counted even beside a Wikipedia text: Berlin 10,730, Hamburg 7,518, Bremen 823. Gedenkbuch 5,880, Yad Vashem 378, USHMM 1,595. Arolsen stays a search.

1945 only in Bremerhaven (month not documented, CC BY 4.0, beside 2025 DOP10). “Today”: official orthophotos with a confirmed GetMap and CORS. Rhineland-Palatinate has no CORS and is not drawn.

Principles

  • No invented or rewritten biography. No text from a language model.
  • Every statement has a source. The inscription is shown as cast. Families can correct a record; the form is not open yet.
  • Licences stay visible. Protected archives are linked, not copied. Anonymised stones stay anonymous.

What we ask

Permission for a sourced excerpt, with author and link, or permission to link only. 1945 aerial photographs with a licence. 3D and photos only if openly licensed. A second check, not new stories. A person to write to about corrections.

How the credit would look. “…the released sentence, word for word…” Source: institution, page title, URL, date of permission. Shown with permission. Licence: as agreed. No credit, no text.
