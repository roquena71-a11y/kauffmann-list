**Subject:** Request for a metadata set / permission – non-profit memorial map "Kauffmann List"

Dear Arolsen Archives team,

I run the **Kauffmann List** (https://roquena71-a11y.github.io/kauffmann-list/), a free, non-profit, cookie-free website that maps 64,576 Stolpersteine in Germany from open data (OpenStreetMap, Wikidata, Wikipedia). We would like to extend it to victims of Nazi persecution and to link each person to the relevant records in your online archive.

**What we request** (index/metadata only, persons recorded as deceased; no document images):
- name, date and place of birth, last known residence, deportation/camp data, date and place of death;
- the permanent link/ID of the corresponding record in your online archive.
We would not copy or re-host digital copies of documents, and we would exclude persons who may still be alive and any special-category information (health, sexuality, criminal records).

**Attribution and linking:** "Source: Arolsen Archives" following your citation format, plus a direct link to the record on each entry and in our data files. We will inform you of the place of publication as your Terms of Use (§7) require.

**Corrections and removal:** we will add a "correct / remove" button to every entry before launch; we would forward corrections to you, apply your updates, and remove entries on your request or on justified objections by relatives (also handled via digitalcollections@its-arolsen.org if you prefer).

**Format:** CSV or JSON (or an API), ideally limited to persons connected to places in Germany, with periodic updates.

**Licence questions:**
1. Under which terms may we republish these metadata in static JSON files that can also be read by other tools, including AI assistants?
2. Does §7 ("not to pass digital copies on to third parties") also apply to index data, and would a written permission cover this use?
3. Which attribution wording do you require?

Our partner data would be stored separately and never merged into the OpenStreetMap-licensed dataset.

Thank you very much for your consideration.
