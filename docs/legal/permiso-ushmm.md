**Subject:** Permission request – non-profit memorial map "Kauffmann List" (Holocaust Survivors and Victims Database)

Dear Reference team,

I run the **Kauffmann List** (https://roquena71-a11y.github.io/kauffmann-list/), a free, non-profit, cookie-free website that maps 64,576 Stolpersteine in Germany from open data (OpenStreetMap, Wikidata, Wikipedia). We plan to extend it to victims of the Holocaust and would like to link and, with your written permission, display limited data from the Holocaust Survivors and Victims Database.

**What we request** (only persons documented as deceased; no Survivors' Registry data and no living persons):
- name, date and place of birth, last residence, deportation/camp, date and place of death;
- the record ID and permalink to your database entry and source list.

**Attribution and linking:** "Source: United States Holocaust Memorial Museum" (unabbreviated) with a direct link on each entry and in our data files; no implied endorsement and no use of your logo.

**Corrections and removal:** we will add a "correct / remove" button to each entry before launch; corrections would be forwarded to you; removal on your request or on justified requests by relatives.

**Format:** CSV or JSON (or an API), preferably limited to persons connected to Germany, with periodic updates.

**Licence and AI questions:**
1. Under which written terms may we republish these fields in static JSON files?
2. Our site offers a machine-readable index (llms.txt / JSON) so that AI assistants can find and cite memorial entries. Your Terms (§II.D.k) prohibit training AI systems without consent. Would you require us to (a) exclude your data from the machine-readable API, (b) mark it with an explicit "no AI training" notice, or (c) could you consent to retrieval-and-citation use (not training)?
3. Which attribution wording do you require?

Your data would be stored in separate files, never merged into the OpenStreetMap-licensed dataset.
