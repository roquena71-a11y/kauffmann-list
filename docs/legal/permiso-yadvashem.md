**Subject:** Permission request – non-profit memorial map "Kauffmann List" (Shoah victims' names data)

Dear Yad Vashem team,

I run the **Kauffmann List** (https://roquena71-a11y.github.io/kauffmann-list/), a free, non-profit, ad-free and cookie-free website that maps 64,576 Stolpersteine in Germany using open data (OpenStreetMap, Wikidata, Wikipedia). We plan to extend it to victims of the Shoah and would like to do this with your permission, not by copying your database.

**What we would like to use** (only for persons recorded as murdered/deceased):
- Name (and maiden name), year/date of birth, place of birth, last residence, deportation (date, destination), place and date of death;
- your record ID and permalink to the Names Database entry.
We do **not** request Pages of Testimony images, submitter data or any data on living persons.

**Attribution and linking:** every entry would show "Source: Yad Vashem – Central Database of Shoah Victims' Names" with a direct link to your record, and our data files would carry the same attribution and your licence terms.

**Corrections and removal:** we will add a "correct / remove" button to each entry before launch; we would forward corrections to you, apply your updates at each refresh, and remove any entry on your request or on a justified request by relatives.

**Format:** CSV or JSON export (or an API) with periodic updates, at whatever scope you consider appropriate (e.g. victims with a last residence in Germany).

**Licence questions:**
1. Under which licence or written terms could we republish these fields, including in static JSON files that other tools (including AI assistants) may read?
2. Do you require any restrictions (e.g. no bulk redistribution, attribution wording, non-commercial only)?
3. Would you prefer that we only link to your records?

The project would keep your data in separate files, never merged into the OpenStreetMap-licensed dataset.

Thank you for your consideration and for your work.
